What is simul8or?

simul8or is a free stock trading simulator. Train decision-making on historical market sessions: test entries and exits, manage risk, and review every trade directly on the chart—no account required.

How the trading simulator works

  • Instant practice: choose a stock and session date, then play, pause, or step through bars.
  • Realistic controls: long/short, market/limit, stops and profit targets with instant fills.
  • Performance tracking: P&L, win rate, average win/loss, equity curve, and on-chart trade review.
  • Skill building: challenges and leaderboards designed for consistent day trading reps.

AI Chart Analyzer

The AI Chart Analyzer accelerates study with AI chart analysis: pattern and momentum context, support/resistance zones, and basic risk-to-reward framing. Use it to turn observations into clear trade plans during replay and live sessions.

AI outputs are educational and not financial advice.

Paper trading

When you want live practice, use our paper trading browser extensions (Chrome and Edge). They enable paper trading on supported sites so you can place simulated orders while prices update—ideal for timing drills without a brokerage account.

Why traders use our simulator

  • No friction: practice immediately—no account or funding required.
  • Fast iteration: instant fills and clear metrics for tight feedback loops.
  • Built for day traders: a focused trading simulator to refine process and risk control.
  • Replay or live: practice historical sessions tick by tick or trade the current market with live prices.
  • Everywhere you are: web app, desktop, and browser extensions for paper trading.

FAQ

Is this a stock market simulator or a trading game?

It’s a realistic stock trading simulator for serious practice and process improvement.

Can I practice with live market data?

Yes. Use the Live Trading Simulator to place simulated trades against current market prices.

Is this paper trading?

The core app uses replay trading with historical sessions. Paper trading is available via our browser extensions.

Do I need a brokerage account?

No. Both replay and live trading use virtual funds, so you can practice day trading without opening or funding a brokerage account.

Can I short and use stops/targets?

Yes—long or short with stops and profit targets for realistic risk management.

What does the AI Chart Analyzer do?

AI-powered chart context—patterns, momentum, support/resistance, and risk-to-reward hints—to speed idea generation.

Is simul8or free?

The core simulator is free. Optional upgrades support continued development.
